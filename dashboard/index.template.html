Stopout is not a remainder

Do not assume dropout equals 100 minus persistence.

Students may transfer, remain enrolled under another status, or leave the observable system. Stopout activates only when a source defines a mutually exclusive cohort outcome.

∅

No wage record is not unemployment

Labor-force status needs its own denominator.

Post-graduation outcomes must retain field of study, award level, cohort, time window, and geography. Missing wage matches cannot be relabeled as unemployment.

How are changes matched?

Period changes require the exact prior interval; annual changes require exactly 12 months, four quarters, or one annual/academic interval. Gaps remain null.

Why are some ranks unavailable?

Raw jobs, GDP size, and student counts mostly reflect county scale. The dashboard compares growth or rates when policy permits and otherwise displays no rank.
