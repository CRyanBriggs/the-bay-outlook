# Limitations

- Unlawful-detainer filings and dispositions are court events, not executed evictions. The source omits unlimited civil cases and cannot observe informal moves or threats.
- Pandemic-era protections and case backlogs affect the court series; a disposition can correspond to an earlier filing.
- ACS estimates pool five survey years, so adjacent releases overlap.
- PIT is a one-night estimate. A sheltered-only cycle can reuse an older unsheltered component.
- HIC measures bed inventory, not occupancy, service quality, exits, or unmet need.
- HUD Picture is not a census of every subsidized or deed-restricted housing program.
- HCD permits are authorizations, not proof of construction, completion, covenant execution, or occupancy.
- RHNA is a planning allocation; the denominator covers a full cycle while the numerator covers 2023–2025.
- FMR is a HUD-area standard, not the county median asking rent.
- Median worker earnings do not describe occupations, households, schedules, or benefits.
- No component set proves causation or individual displacement. Version 1.2 intentionally produces no composite risk score or county ranking.
