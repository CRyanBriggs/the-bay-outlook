# Limitations

- ACS five-year vintages overlap. Consecutive releases are not independent annual samples.
- ACS values are estimates subject to sampling and nonsampling error. Margins of error are retained, but they do not capture every source of uncertainty.
- The project reliability flag is a review heuristic, not an official Census classification.
- B25140A–I begins in 2023; the burden-by-race series is not backcast.
- Race and Hispanic-origin iterations overlap. They cannot be summed to a county total.
- Householder characteristics describe occupied housing units and do not characterize every household member.
- “No spouse present” is a table category, not a complete description of family structure.
- Education-tenure and education-earnings tables have different universes and are not linked records.
- ACS occupation earnings are residence-based medians, not employer wage offers or workplace-county earnings.
- HUD FMR is a modeled area rent standard, not an asking-rent median or proof that a unit is available.
- A 30% benchmark does not account for taxes, household size, debt, transportation, childcare, disability-related costs, or preferences.
- LODES JT01 describes primary jobs, not unique households. Values are noise-infused and can differ from ACS employment estimates.
- Workplace job inflows do not measure a person’s trip frequency, travel mode, or lived commute burden.
- County aggregation conceals city, neighborhood, and within-group variation.
- Descriptive differences do not establish discrimination, policy effects, displacement mechanisms, or causation.
- No county ranking, composite equity score, forecast, or automated narrative conclusion is produced.
