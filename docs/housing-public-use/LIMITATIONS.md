# Limitations

- “Latest” differs by source and measure. Version 1.5 preserves the observation period beside each value.
- County comparisons are descriptive; they do not control for population, land area, household composition, policy regime, or economic structure unless a measure explicitly says so.
- Redfin remains a Tier 2 private primary-market source and is not treated as a government statistic.
- ACS five-year estimates overlap across vintages and carry sampling uncertainty.
- LODES records jobs, not unique people, and uses disclosure protection.
- HUD Fair Market Rent is a program benchmark, not a county median asking rent.
- Court filing data describe limited civil filings, not unique displaced households or final case outcomes.
- RHNA allocations, annual permits, and annual completions are distinct concepts; annual stages are not treated as one project cohort.
- Zoning composition is not legal capacity or a policy-performance score.
- Race and Hispanic-origin table iterations overlap and are not additive.
